Questions Cloud VPS questions Is there a trial period or money-back guarantee? There is no trial period or money-back guarantee on VPS hosting. Cloud VPS is billed month to month, and you can cancel at any time from your account. Can I pay quarterly or yearly? Cloud VPS is billed monthly. You can prepay credit to your InterServer account if you want future invoices covered automatically, but there is no quarterly or yearly discount. Can I add more slices later? Yes. You can increase the number of slices as the VPS needs more CPU, memory, storage, or transfer. Start small if you are unsure, then scale when usage justifies it. When is managed support included? Managed support is included on VPS plans with 8 slices or more. Support can help diagnose server issues, failed services, database problems, control panel issues, and common performance concerns. Should I choose VPS or shared hosting? Choose shared hosting when you mainly need website hosting, email, a control panel, and support without managing a server.
