FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

What's the difference between Regular CPU and High Frequency CPU?
Both run on the same NVMe storage and 40 Gbps network. High Frequency CPU runs on 4.2 GHz+ AMD Ryzen cores, better for single-threaded workloads like game servers, trading bots, and web apps with low parallelism. Pricing is identical, so most users pick High Frequency by default.

How does the 50% annual discount work?
Pay for 12 months upfront and you get the same plan for half the monthly rate. No auto-renew, you'll get an invoice before each renewal so you can downgrade, upgrade, or cancel without surprises. The 14-day money-back guarantee still applies on annual plans.

Are there any hidden fees? Egress, IPs, snapshots?
No. The price you see includes the full transfer allotment, IPv4 + IPv6, and root access. Snapshots are free. Outbound traffic is metered against your monthly transfer allotment, exceed it and we'll alert you instead of throttling. Extra IPs and floating IPs are listed separately.

Can I upgrade later without rebuilding?
Yes. RAM, vCPU, and storage can be resized live from the panel, most upgrades complete in under 60 seconds with no downtime. You can also clone an instance to a larger plan if you'd rather migrate gradually.

What payment methods do you accept?
Major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx), PayPal, and crypto (BTC, ETH, USDT). Wire transfer is available for annual contracts over $1,000.

Do you offer GPUs for AI training?
Yes. We have Nvidia A100 (1× / 2× / 4×) for high-end training, RTX 5090 for newer workloads, and RTX 4090 as the cost-effective option. All come with the same 40 Gbps network and pre-installed CUDA images. See the Premium GPU table above.

What's a Floating IP and when do I need one?
A Floating IP is a static IPv4 you can attach and detach from any of your instances at will. Useful for failover (move the IP to a backup server during maintenance), blue/green deploys, or pinning a public IP across rebuilds. $2.50/IP/month.

Money-back guarantee, what's the catch?
There isn't one. Cancel within 14 days for any reason and we refund the full amount, no questions asked. The only exclusion is repeated abuse-of-policy refunds (multiple new accounts taking advantage of the trial repeatedly).
