Choosing the right hosting path

Cloud VPS is a middle step in cloud hosting: more control than shared hosting, less hardware management than a dedicated server.

Good fit Custom applications, private databases, web apps, staging servers, higher traffic sites, and isolated workloads.
Confirm first You are comfortable managing an operating system or you are choosing 8+ slices for managed support.
Use dedicated when The workload needs exclusive hardware, unusual network requirements, or sustained resource use beyond VPS scale.
