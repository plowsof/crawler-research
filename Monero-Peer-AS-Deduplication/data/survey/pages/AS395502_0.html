Services

Internet Connectivity

Sunbreak Electronics features a fully redundant network throughout all edge, core, and distribution layers. The facility has multi-homed network bandwidth and carrier-neutral cross connects are available to national Tier 1 providers. We currently have 17 on-net data centers across North America providing ultra low latency paths.

Colocation

Sunbreak Electronics colocation allows you to deploy and manage your existing infrastructure in our data center while eliminating the hassle and cost of maintaining your own facility. Our unique service offering allows you to define your own redundancy with 8 diverse power feeds, passive cooling, and resilient network connectivity giving you greater uptime and reliability and the peace of mind to know your equipment is in trusted hands.

Cloud Connectivity

Sunbreak Electronics makes it easy for you to build a hybrid cloud solution. In our facility we provide secure direct connections to leading cloud providers such as Google, Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services (AWS) enabling you to deploy a fast and flexible cloud solution.

Data Center Information

All the important details you need to know

  • Carriers

    The facility has diverse fiber optic paths from the following carriers connecting directly to our facility: Cogent, Astound, Hurricane Electric, Wholesail, Lumen (Centurylink/Level 3), Verizon, AT&T, NWAX, Megaport and Packet Fabric.

  • Environmental

    The facility is passively cooled to ASHRAE Class "A1" environemntal standards usign MERV 11 air filtration and seasonal evaporative cooling (June - September). This combination makes it highly improbable to have a failure related to overheating. The temperature in the data center space is maintained ibetween 67F - 82F with the typical temperature being 72F.

  • Security

    The facility entrance opens to a man trap and security check in. Our building was built above the 100 year flood plain on a reinforced concrete slab. The facility is monitored 24/7 by security systems with cellular and satellite based reporting. The premises are monitored via high resolution IP cameras with night vision capability. Video is recorded 24-hours a day to servers that are in a secure off-site location.

  • Power

    The facility has 8 diverse power feeds fed from 8 distinct transformers that have entry points on the East and West side of our building.
