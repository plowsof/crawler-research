Raising Kids in the Digital Age Just Got Easier

Whole Home Mesh Wi-Fi System

As our lives are becoming more and more connected, we rely more than ever on having access to a fast Internet connection wherever we are. Yet, when it comes to home Wi-Fi, the experience can be frustrating, such as when you’re trying to watch your favorite show – just to be greeted with a buffer bar. This is especially true if you live in a home where Wi-Fi dead spots are common, restricting you to work or enjoy entertainment in rooms with a strong signal.

CentraCom now offers a new mesh Wi-Fi router we are calling CentraWiFi Blast to eliminate dead spots in your home. For larger homes, mesh extenders can be added giving a strong signal throughout the home (our technicians will help determine if extenders are needed in your home).
