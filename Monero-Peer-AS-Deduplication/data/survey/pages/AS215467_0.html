Why Skhron?

Skhron is a sovereign and privacy-first European hosting provider, offering KVM virtual servers in Europe. We operate our own network and rely on own hardware, delivering 99.95% measured uptime, backed by a generous SLA. Full KVM virtualization and custom ISO come standard on all plans – your server is under your full control, properly isolated from other customers.

Internet as it should be – peer-to-peer! We include a routed IPv6 /64 prefix with every server, and a free /56 available on request. Free BGP sessions are available in all locations.

We accept Bitcoin, Bitcoin Lightning, Monero, Tron, Bitcoin Cash, and other cryptocurrencies through a self-hosted, non-custodial payment processor – no middlemen, no third-party tracking, no seized funds. Traditional payments via Visa, Mastercard, and SEPA are available as well.

No KYC is required to order or use our services. Founded in Ukraine and registered in Estonia, Skhron has served hundreds of customers since 2023 with a proven track record of transparent pricing, responsive support, and infrastructure you can trust.
