Services

An overview of the infrastructure we operate and the areas where we can offer support.

Domain Registration

Domain registration across more than 1,500 TLDs. This covers everything from common extensions to country-code and specialised TLDs used by projects that need a consistent, stable presence on the web.

DNS Hosting

Authoritative DNS for projects and services. Anycast resolution, support for modern record types, and no reliance on third-party DNS providers that may have conflicting interests.

Content Delivery Network

CDN infrastructure to serve content efficiently without routing it through large commercial platforms. Useful for projects that want to stay independent of major cloud providers.

VPS & Dedicated Servers

Virtual machines and dedicated hardware for projects that need compute. Both are available depending on what a project requires, and we are comfortable working with whatever stack is already in place.

VPNs

VPN infrastructure for projects and individuals who care about keeping their traffic private. No logging policies driven by advertising, no upselling. Just connectivity.

IP Transit

Upstream connectivity for networks that need it. We peer broadly and care about where traffic goes. Routing decisions are made with the health of the wider internet in mind, not just cost.

Managed Services

Running infrastructure is not the right focus for every project. We can take on the operational side: monitoring, updates, incident response, and day-to-day management, so the people behind a project can spend their time on the work that matters to them.

Not sure whether we can help?

If you are working on something that aligns with what we do, or you are not sure whether it does, reach out anyway.
